    Feed & place attribution

    Which town each feed and place belongs to — this decides whose news line and town tile it appears under. Three rules:

    • Main town — counted with the main town. If the main town is changed later, these items stay with the old main town (pinned automatically, so nothing silently moves).
    • An extra town — pinned to that town, always.
    • Removing a town deletes its feeds, their stories, and its places. The main town can't be removed.
